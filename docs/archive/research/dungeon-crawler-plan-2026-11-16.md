# The Silt Archive — standalone dungeon crawler plan

**Status: proposal / research, not implemented.** This is a distinct, independent game family, not a Cinder Vault content pack. Cinder currently offers three 7–9-room wings, three classes, seven enemy archetypes, six weapons, twelve items, nine recipes, exploration UI, and complete death/victory/restart. The target below matches or exceeds that breadth with different decisions: compact room-scale tactical exploration, a rising tide, and an extraction choice rather than shooter movement and warden-gated descents.

## Pitch and run

You are a diver retrieving records from a submerged municipal archive. Each expedition assembles **three districts of 8–10 rooms** from authored chambers. Find the seal fragments in each district to open its lift; recover the Heart Register in the third and return to that district's lift to win. Each district boss guards one of its two fragments. At each lift, extract early to bank discoveries as a partial success or continue deeper for the full ending. Dying loses carried rewards; a run ends at extraction, full victory, or death, with an explicit result and restart. Target 25–40 minutes; this is a playtest goal, not an established measurement.

The central choice is **water level**. Every accepted move, attack, use, or wait advances one turn; inspection, inventory navigation and rejected commands do not. A district starts at water 0; every 8 accepted turns it rises by one, capped at 4. Water affects marked tiles only: at 1 submerged channels become difficult terrain; at 2 flooded tiles impair movement and conduct lightning; at 3 vents activate and water enemies strengthen; at 4 air drains by 1 after each turn while standing on a flooded tile. A pump lowers the district by 2 once, never below zero. Tide is visible, predictable, and never a hard timer: on dry tiles air does not drain, and air can be restored with supplies. All chambers have at least one dry traversable route to required objectives at all tide levels. Movement on difficult flooded tiles costs one air rather than another turn, with the cost shown before committing; zero air causes 2 health damage per air owed, not a locked movement. Thus the player can always leave, albeit at risk. Tide remains at its last value on revisits; moving to a new district starts its own clock.

## Spatial and combat rules

- Orthographic top-down, one explorer on a 9×9 discrete grid per chamber; cardinal movement, solid walls, doors at matching edge sockets. Rooms keep enemies, opened caches, pumps and hazards on revisit. Door transitions cost a turn and preserve district tide. The map marks visited rooms and revealed neighboring silhouettes; it does not reveal enemies/loot in unvisited rooms. Entry into a room is always safe from immediate damage until the next accepted action.
- Each accepted player action resolves player effect, then living alerted enemies in stable spawn-ID order, then terrain/tide effects and terminal checks. Illegal target, blocked door, empty item, insufficient resources, full bag and paused input do not spend a turn or change state. Death immediately stops remaining resolutions. Ranged attacks require a clear cardinal line; melee requires cardinal adjacency. Telegraphs are explicit tiles and text, not just color. Navigation queries are advisory and enemy movement rechecks current occupancy.
- Enemies enter an alert state on room entry; their intended next action is displayed before commitment. Basic move/attack does not require AP or real-time aiming. Player has health and air plus **charge** (0–6); attacks build one charge on hit, tools consume it. Heavy strike spends 2 charge and breaks armor; insulated arc spends 3, targets up to 3 aligned tiles, and chains once to an enemy on a connected flooded tile; brace spends 1 and halves the next hit. Check valid target/range and costs before execution. No extra implicit cooldowns.
- Three starting kits: **Surveyor** (balanced blade, map clue to nearest fragment), **Wrecksmith** (heavy tool, starts with repair scrap), **Current Reader** (arc tool, air reserve). All can obtain other tools. Level XP from encounters and first-time discoveries uses a linear provider curve; each level offers a deterministic choice from three local upgrades, with queued choices resolved before the next turn. Sample upgrades: extra air, charged attack damage, reduced water damage, first brace per room free, cache insight, and extra heal from supplies. Class identity is initial equipment and a passive, not exclusive content.

## Dungeon assembly and solvability

New game-local seeded graph assembly is necessary: the existing `dungeon/generator` makes a shooter `WorldDescription` from a **content** seed, not run-seeded independent-family chambers. For each district assemble 8–10 nodes on a bounded grid from compatible 9×9 authored room templates with N/E/S/W door sockets. Create a connected spanning path from entry through two distinct fragment rooms to lift, attach 2–4 optional branches, and optionally join compatible adjacent sockets for one loop. Choose compatible template rotations, assign encounters and rewards with the run's seeded gameplay RNG, and validate the actual tiles, door positions, dry paths, keys, and objective order. Use bounded retries followed by a validated authored fallback layout; do not silently ship an impossible seed.

Both fragments are local objective flags, not inventory items, and are guaranteed in reachable distinct non-entry rooms; the exit door checks both. No mandatory combat drop, randomly placed key, consumable, or optional branch is required to proceed. The final lift requires the Register as well; it is awarded by defeating the third-district boss at its authored arena, after which the player returns along the mapped route. A lift extraction choice is available on arrival in the entry room and after unlocking the district exit; final victory is only registered by extracting with the Register. Lift interaction and confirmation are free UI actions; leaving the interface cannot advance the tide. Doorways remain open after unlock and do not trap the player. Each generated layout has stable IDs and stable ordering; a displayed run seed plus the same content version and command sequence reproduces layout, encounters and rewards, with cosmetic RNG isolated. A normal retry draws a fresh run seed, while a seed-entry retry explicitly repeats it; implement through a deterministic run-local seed derived from the host's seeded RNG, without global randomness.

## Outcomes and persistence

On partial extraction bank discovered archive entries and record deepest district; the result explicitly says **Extracted**, not victory. On death bank only previously banked profile entries; show unbanked discoveries lost and cause. On final extraction bank carried entries and record **Victory**. All outcomes show seed, district, turns, discoveries and restart/menu; persist once per terminal transition, never on repeat ticks. Profile uses versioned namespaced keys, validates corrupt values as defaults, and storage failure leaves a playable run with a visible non-blocking notice. No in-progress save is promised. Restart disposes old owned handles and creates a fresh state; seed-repeat is an explicit separate option.

## Reuse decisions and implementation boundaries

Discovery was run with Node 22.19 using `find-reuse` queries for seeded rooms, inventory, room maps, graph paths, XP and resource/cooldown; `--show` inspected `dungeon/generator`, `mechanics/room-map`, `recipe/stack-inventory`, `recipe/resource-actions` and `recipe/ordered-navigation`. Also consulted `docs/reference/reuse-index.md`, SDK contracts, provider READMEs, Hollowmere consumer and inventory tests.

| Need | Candidate ID | Decision and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| World generation | `dungeon/generator` | Do not depend on it: shooter-only static content-seeded map, max three isolated wings, no runtime swapping, socket templates or per-run topology. Implement bounded game-local assembly for this concrete gap; consider extraction to a shared provider only with a second real consumer and conformance evidence. |
| Room discovery | `mechanics/room-map` | Reuse `RoomMapRole` / `roomMapMechanic` for current, visited and neighbor reveal; generated graph must satisfy its rectangle/link validation. It does not generate rooms, verify gates, render, or persist. Use one owned map per district, so revisits preserve fog. |
| Movement/pursuit | `mechanics/navigation`, `recipe/ordered-navigation` | Reuse unit-edge search for legal adjacency and enemy pursuit, with stable neighbor order; local occupancy, transitions and air costs remain authoritative. No weighted pathfinding or mutation. |
| Turn economy | `recipe/resource-actions`, `mechanics/resources`, `mechanics/actions`, `mechanics/clock`, `mechanics/objectives`, `mechanics/kits` | Reuse charge/air/health transactions, explicit turn clock and fragment objectives when semantics fit. Validate targets first; clock advances exactly once per accepted decision. Provider actions do not rollback local effects, so prevalidate and only apply infallible local effects after successful commit. No clamping assumed: compute bounded gain explicitly. Tide schedule and damage policy stay local. |
| Pack and crafting | `recipe/stack-inventory`, `mechanics/inventory`, `recipe/known-crafting`, `mechanics/crafting` | Reuse stack/equipment handle and atomic item recipes; inventory has no per-instance durability, weight, effects, item drop, persistence or cross-inventory transactions. Effects and cache state are local. |
| Levels | `recipe/experience-levels`, `mechanics/progression` | Reuse XP/linear levels/pending ledger; offer and apply local upgrades, then consume each pending reward. No automatic perk or save system. |
| Input, terminal persistence, rendering | `recipe/bounded-command-queue`, `recipe/scoped-input`, `recipe/profile-storage`, `recipe/owned-3d-presentation`, `recipe/semantic-audio` | Compose owned ingress, contexts, Profile and optional Ui/Render/Audio. Never advance simulation while paused; presentation consumes detached observations and sends semantic commands. |
| Cinder gameplay/camera | `cinder/game`, `core/combat`, `presentation/camera-modes` | Family-bound Cinder/shooter rules and camera assume shooter world/player; do not import or claim they support this standalone board. Reference for UX and lifecycle only. |

Proposed plugins: `silt/game` registers experience and game content kinds, owns run state, validation and SDK `Silt` observations/commands; `silt/content` contains declarative templates, enemies, items, upgrades and kit; `silt/presentation` selects HUD and implements the grid, map, input and accessible panels; an existing theme can provide verified assets where suitable. Add SDK contract in `packages/sdk/src/contracts/` and export via index; contract contains detached state, bounded commands and explicit result, not implementation references. Enable providers and choose roles in a new preset and register compiled plugins in `src/app/catalog.ts`. Content loader dependencies must be explicit; selected inventory/progression/crafting/kit factory roles need `roleDependencies`, while pure navigation and room-map roles use selection requirements according to their actual provider semantics. No plugin imports another plugin or kernel. Resolve owner-bound factories in activation, own every handle/context and clean up on restart; simulation operates headlessly. Report content errors with source paths and actionable messages. Any new shared capability requires its design/evidence note, metadata and reuse-index refresh; do not make this game-local graph generator cross-genre by assertion.

## Phases and acceptance gates

1. **Foundation:** define game contract, minimal content validation, preset/role graph and detached state, start/restart lifecycle. Headless stage/activate/stop; verify paused command rejection and no leaked handles. Avoid presentation-first coupling.
2. **Generation and exploration:** implement template validation, seeded assembly, fallback, map and tile transitions. Headless property suite over a fixed corpus of hundreds of seeds and all difficulties: deterministic same seed, differing topology for selected seeds, valid links/sockets, reachable fragments/lift and dry paths at each water level; verify backtracking preserves room state and failed generation uses valid fallback. Seed reproducibility test includes identical command replay.
3. **Turns/combat:** explicit player/enemy/environment ordering, line-of-sight, intent, tide and air, all archetypes and bosses. Tests check every failed command consumes nothing, accepted commands tick exactly once, deterministic tie breaks, telegraph-to-effect, dry escape, pump bounds, air-zero damage and terminal short-circuit. Exercise one complete actual combat encounter without damage shortcuts.
4. **Progression/content:** implement all listed rooms, three districts, encounters, upgrade choices, pack/crafting, events and unlock requirements. Validate every content reference, item slot, recipe and spawn, and test full-bag/crafting atomicity, queued XP choices, route guarantees and each boss phase. Test a complete three-district win, early extraction and death with profile success/failure/corruption, restart and seed repeat.
5. **Presentation/polish:** keyboard play and pointer commands for movement, targeting, lift, inventory, crafting and map; display selected kit, turn, water cadence, air, charge, objective, enemy intents, legible hazard preview, seed and result. Focus-managed panels have text labels, non-color-only intent and narrow-screen layouts. Audio uses semantic cues and tested asset provenance. Production browser acceptance at desktop and phone sizes: select kit and difficulty, move/fight, inspect map, inventory/craft, revisit, use pump, extract and restart; run a full win and death route, disclosing any automation acceleration separately and retaining a normal combat path. Capture screenshots and browser console errors; manually assess pointer/keyboard feel and balance.
6. **Integration:** update README, SPEC, ARCHITECTURE system tables, content format and a player guide; add plugin metadata/recipes if reusable behavior changed. Run `npm run index-reuse`, `npm run check`, `npm run validate-game -- --bundled`, `npm run build`, `npm run smoke` and the new browser demo; record actual outcomes and environmental limitations. Include one independent selected provider substitution where available for composed mechanics; no claim of balance before playtesting.
