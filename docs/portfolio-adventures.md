# Two portfolio adventures

Both games use the point-and-click `story/adventure` rules, `story/presentation`, and the existing `theme/story`. Choose highlighted actions with the mouse or Tab and Enter. The journal records clues; Save, Load and Start over use the existing chapter-specific story controls.

## The Quiet Index

An archivist has concealed a register that may save a river community from demolition. Investigate the closed stacks, repair the damaged document and decide whether to publish the names or entrust them privately to the residents' advocate. The two conversations with Iva give different accounts of her silence; either can lead to either ending.

**Route:** ask or challenge Iva; enter the stacks; examine the chart and make a rubbing; set the cabinet to REED; visit the bindery, take tissue and repair the fragment; return through the stacks and reading room to the hearing; choose a recipient for the register.

## The Night Orchard

On a seedship, the last pear tree is dying from salt in its water. Follow the maintenance and propagation clues to save a cutting, then choose whether the one remaining wake charge goes to a botanist or an engineer. Each ending protects the cutting but leaves a different task for the future crew.

**Route:** visit the cistern, read the diagram, retrieve the filter and draw water; visit the nursery, read the manual and take the knife; cut a shoot in the orchard; return to the nursery and soak it; visit the sleeper gallery and wake Sen or Sol. The nursery and cistern can be visited in either order.

## Reuse and limitations

`story/adventure` supplies the room graph, gated actions, item combinations, choices, journal and replay-based saves; `story/presentation` supplies the interface and `theme/story` supplies existing artwork. The stories are authored content, not new mechanics. `mechanics/inventory` was considered but is a stack/equipment provider rather than the family-specific unique-item action model. The reused theme shows observatory imagery rather than bespoke archive or spacecraft illustrations. Each chapter's victory screen has one shared epilogue, while the chosen ending is recorded in its action text and journal. Both presets and content packs are registered in the bundled catalog.
