# Two portfolio adventures

Both games use the `story/adventure` room, inventory and choice rules with `story/presentation` and `theme/story`. Choose actions with the mouse or Tab and Enter. Unavailable actions explain the missing items or that another clue is required; the journal records discoveries. Save, Load and Start over use chapter-specific story controls. The story response and chosen ending are shown beneath the scene, before the satchel and journal in reading order.

## The Quiet Index

Tomorrow the city plans to demolish the river quarter. Find and restore the missing register, establish its connection to the threatened land, and listen to the residents before choosing how much to disclose. Asking or challenging Iva records different accounts of her silence; neither determines the ending.

**Route:** speak to Iva and enter the stacks. Examine the chart, make a rubbing and open the cabinet. Inspect the survey index; in the bindery, find survey 14, take tissue and restore the fragment. Take both documents to the hearing, ask the residents and compare the records. Choose to publish the names, file privately, or publish the boundary while sealing the names. Each choice suspends demolition pending a hearing, but has different costs for privacy and public scrutiny.

## The Night Orchard

A seedship's pear tree is dying from salt in its water. Prepare a cutting, read the specialists' logs and decide whether to wake a botanist, wake an engineer, or preserve the wake charge and undertake a manual watch yourself. None resolves every problem.

**Route:** in the cistern, read the diagram, test the line, retrieve the filter and draw water after flushing it. In the nursery, read the manual and take the knife; cut a living shoot in the orchard and soak it in the nursery. Visit the sleeper gallery and read the duty logs before choosing Sen or Sol. For the third option, also read the caretaker's reserve-power log in the orchard and set the manual bypass in the cistern. The cistern and nursery can be visited in either order; the bypass needs both the power log and the diagram. The manual watch keeps the cutting supplied only with continued effort and leaves the pump broken.

## Reuse and limitations

`story/adventure` supplies the room graph, gated actions, unique-item combinations, choices, journal and replay-based saves; `story/presentation` supplies the interface. `mechanics/inventory` is a stack/equipment provider, not this family-specific action model; `mechanics/room-map` does not supply story transitions or gated actions. The stories use existing declarative content rather than adding either provider. The chapter versions were raised because new prerequisites change replay legality, so older saves for these two chapters are incompatible.

The existing pinned Assetrizz Story artwork is available through `theme/story` and appears as the scene plate and selected item icons. It depicts an observatory and a brass key, rather than bespoke archive or seedship art. Chapter-specific archive and orchard artwork would require additional theme keys and asset/provenance work outside these content packs. The shared completion panel repeats the selected ending's authored text; its general epilogue and the journal remain available after further exploration. Both presets and content packs are registered in the bundled catalog.
