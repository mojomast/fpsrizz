# Salt Ledger rules

`salt/game` registers the `salt-campaign` content kind, the `salt/ledger` experience and the `SaltLedger` service. Select navigation, progression, objectives, inventory and relationships mechanic roles and supply `options.campaign`. The simulation uses an owned bounded queue and runs without a UI.

`state().journal` exposes eight tracked three-stage side quests: status, completed stage count, current action ID, location, instruction and reward. Send `interact` with the current action at the specified location; four chains unlock when another is completed. Rewards are issued once. `quests.ts` defines the story interactions and items. The legacy `campaign.quests` prompts remain available.

Five earned equipment pieces add range, damage or armor. `use` names the actor in `id`; outside combat, `target: 'healing-draught'` heals that member. In combat `target` is `item:target-id`: healing draught heals a living ally within two cells, brine tonic transfers one AP to another living ally within two cells, and salt bomb deals five damage to an enemy within three cells. Each costs one AP and consumes one item only after validation. Market smugglers, the cairn gale and basilica echo are optional one-time encounters with consumable rewards. Progress and inventory are session-only; reset and restart begin afresh.

Battles expose `terrain` cells in the detached state snapshot. Cover at (2,0) reduces attacks from more than one cell away by one damage (minimum one), for either side; bombs are unaffected. The obstacle at (2,1) blocks both sides' movement and is excluded from the graph passed to the selected navigation provider. The hazard at (2,2) deals one damage to each living occupant after enemy actions, before victory or defeat is resolved. A simultaneous party and enemy wipe is a defeat. Terrain is reset on each encounter and retry.

`state().bonds` contains detached symmetric pairs for recruited members, with scores 0–2. At the Ledger Hall, outside battle, send `{type:'bond', id:actorId, target:otherActorId}` to spend one `state().bondTraining` and raise that pair by one. Both members must be recruited; inactive party members can train. One training is initially available; each one-time encounter victory recovers one, up to three. Retry, defeat and repeat victory attempts grant none. A fully trained pair cannot consume training. During battle a normal attack or damaging ability gains the highest bond score with any living orthogonally adjacent ally; bonuses do not stack. Healing and bombs do not benefit. Bonds and training reset with reset or restart; no save/load is provided.
