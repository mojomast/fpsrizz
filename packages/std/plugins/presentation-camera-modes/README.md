# First-person and isometric camera modes

`presentation/camera-modes` is a shooter-family `CameraRole` provider. Enable it and select `"sdk/camera": "presentation/camera-modes"` in place of the first-person camera; [Cinder Vault](../../../../games/cinder/presets/cinder-vault.json) pairs it with the shooter world and player-avatar presenter.

It publishes `View` and optional `ViewModes`: `set`, `toggle`, `rotate` and `pickGround`, with `ViewModeChanged` on a mode change. Default keys are O to toggle and Z/X to rotate; FOV, orthographic size, camera position, yaw and keys are configurable in the [manifest](manifest.json). The isometric camera follows the player and shows a floor marker. [Core presentation](../../../../games/shooter/plugins/core-presentation/index.ts) adapts ceilings and first-person visuals; the optional [avatar](../presentation-player-avatar/README.md) adds a body.

The scoped render camera lease and owned marker are cleaned up with the provider. Headless operation tolerates absent render/input services. This is an orthographic projection of the existing 3D shooter world, not a general 2D camera; ground picking is a plane raycast, with no pan, zoom or cutaway shader. See [camera guide](../../../../docs/reference/camera-modes.md), [SDK](../../../sdk/src/contracts/viewmodes.ts), [Cinder tests](../../../../games/cinder/tests/cinder-vault.test.ts), [render tests](../../../../tests/render-three.test.ts) and [browser demo](../../../../games/cinder/tests/cinder-demo/run.mjs).
